About

Adventure has always been a way of discovering what is possible.

I’m Charlotte Kirk, an endurance athlete and adventurer whose sporting life has taken me across continents, over mountain ranges and along some of the world’s most demanding trails.

From running the Pacific Crest Trail to setting a fastest known time on the Camino de Santiago, I’m drawn to challenges that demand resilience, self-belief and the willingness to keep moving when the outcome is uncertain.
