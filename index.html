Dr Charlotte Kirk is a technology investor and operator specialising in the clean energy transition. She was previously the Head of Technology at Fortescue, leading strategy and investment for a $6.2 billion industrial electrification program across renewable generation, battery storage, transmission and zero-emission mining equipment and operations. She subsequently led European investments at Clean Energy Ventures. She has a PhD in Chemistry from Oxford University, where she was an Academic Scholar engineering new battery materials and renewable fuels.

She was also the first female in the British Army's infantry where she served for 5 years, and is a North Face-sponsored endurance athlete competing at World Championships across ultramarathons, Ironmans and cross-country skiing. She has set the Fastest Known Time running the 800 km Camino de Santiago and ran the 4,500 km Pacific Crest Trail as 90 consecutive ultramarathons. These experiences shape how she thinks about resilience, leadership and execution under pressure.
